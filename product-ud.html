UD Carbon Fabric

Unidirectional carbon fiber fabric for construction reinforcement
and seismic retrofit applications.

Product Overview

FTC UD carbon fabric is listed in the catalogue for construction reinforcement and seismic retrofit applications.

The catalogue specifies TAIRYFIL TC-36P-12K carbon fiber, 50 cm fabric width and 100 m packing length per roll for the listed reinforcement products.

ECCF is listed at 200 g/m² and ECCH at 300 g/m². Other specifications can be customized based on request.
